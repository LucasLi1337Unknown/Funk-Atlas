03 / READ THE FINE PRINT

Honest numbers.

This is a research snapshot of the PASSO BEM SOLTO era, from 23 April 2024 to 22 October 2026. It is an evolving discovery catalog, not a complete history or an official chart.

Download the dataset ↗
How are songs counted?

Speed and reverb variants are merged when their base title and at least one credited artist match. Sequels and named remixes stay separate. Each displayed artist receives one appearance per song.

What do the credits mean?

They are displayed artist credits from Spotify, not a complete list of songwriters, producers or sample creators. Edition credits are combined after merging variants. Same-name artists can remain ambiguous.

What are the limitations?

Playlist inclusion is discovery evidence, not proof of virality or genre. Current playlists cannot reconstruct every historical trend. Embeds expose up to 100 entries per playlist. Dates may refer to reissues. Older candidates are retained, but their revival dates are unverified. Explicitly named hardstyle, hardtekk and jumpstyle tracks were excluded; genre classification is otherwise provisional. This snapshot does not update automatically.
